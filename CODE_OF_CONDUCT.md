# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

AI Scaffold is intended to be a practical, respectful, contribution-friendly project. Everyone participating in issues, pull requests, discussions, documentation, reviews, and related community spaces is expected to help keep the project welcoming and useful.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent, but accept correction when impact differs from intent.
- Give actionable feedback with enough context for others to improve.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.
- Credit sources and prior work where appropriate.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberately derailing issues or pull requests.
- Spam, bad-faith contributions, or intentionally misleading claims.
- Attempts to bypass the project license or attribution requirements.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or block participants when behavior harms the project or community.
